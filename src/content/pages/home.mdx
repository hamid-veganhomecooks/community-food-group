## How It Works

Once a month we get together, cook, and then take the food out and hand it to people directly - including at street locations like bus stops.

We do not publish a time or a place in advance. The arrangements are made close to the date and they genuinely change, so anything printed here would be wrong more often than it was right. Write to [info@vegansagainstfascism.org](mailto:info@vegansagainstfascism.org) and a person will write back with the details.

There is one place we can name: a plot we rent at a community garden, which grows some of what we cook.
