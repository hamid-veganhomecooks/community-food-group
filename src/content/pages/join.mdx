# Cook With Us

There is no form to fill in, no sign-up, and no membership scheme. If you want to cook with us, or to help hand food out, the way in is to say so.

Write to info@GROUP_DOMAIN and tell us you would like to come along. A person reads that address and will write back to arrange it. It is not an application and there is nothing to pass - and it is not automatic either, so give someone a little time to answer.

That address is the only way to reach us. We do not run a group chat, and there is no chat link on this site.

## What There Is to Do

Two things happen, and you are welcome at either or both.

### Cooking

We get together and cook. Everything we make is vegan, so nothing you cook with us will contain meat, dairy or eggs.

### Handing food out

We take what we cooked out around town and give it to people directly, including at street locations like bus stops.

Both happen monthly at best effort. The time and the place are settled close to the date and they change, which is why they are not published here - it is the email that gets you the details.

## If You Cannot Make a Cook Session

Ingredients, containers, kitchen time and transport are all as useful as an afternoon of cooking. The [ways to help](/help) page sets out what we need and how to offer it.

We are also putting cooking classes together, about cheap, healthy meals. They are not running yet. If you would come to one, or you could teach, mention it when you write.
