# Join Us

Whether you have an hour a week or a special skill to share, we'd love to have you on our team.

## Volunteer Opportunities

### Food Distribution
Help us sort and distribute fresh produce to community members. This is our most hands-on role, perfect for those who love meeting new people.

- **Time commitment:** 2-4 hours per week
- **Skills needed:** None - we'll train you!
- **Schedule:** Flexible shifts available

### Workshop Facilitators
Share your knowledge by leading cooking demos, nutrition workshops, or gardening sessions.

- **Time commitment:** Varies by workshop
- **Skills needed:** Expertise in cooking, nutrition, or gardening
- **Schedule:** Flexible based on availability

### Administrative Support
Help with coordination, outreach, and day-to-day operations.

- **Time commitment:** 3-5 hours per week
- **Skills needed:** Strong organization and communication
- **Schedule:** Remote or in-person options

## Become a Member

As a member, you'll receive:
- Weekly updates on local food initiatives
- Invitations to community events
- Access to our member-only cooking classes
- A voice in our organization's direction

**Membership is free** - we believe in breaking down barriers to participation.

## Sign Up

Ready to join? Reach out to us through our social channels or attend one of our upcoming events. We can't wait to meet you!
