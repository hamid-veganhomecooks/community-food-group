# Donate

Your support makes our work possible. Every donation, whether monetary or in-kind, helps us reach more community members in need.

## Ways to Donate

### Monetary Donations

Your financial contributions help us:
- Purchase fresh produce when supply is low
- Rent kitchen spaces for cooking workshops
- Cover transportation costs for food distributions
- Fund educational materials and resources

**Every dollar counts** - even small donations add up to create big impact.

### Food Donations

We accept donations of:
- Fresh produce (from gardens, farms, or markets)
- Non-perishable pantry staples
- Prepared meals (from restaurants or catering events)
- Seeds and gardening supplies

**Please ensure** all food donations are fresh, unopened, and within their use-by dates.

### Equipment & Supplies

We're always in need of:
- Kitchen equipment (pots, pans, utensils)
- Storage containers and shelving
- Transportation boxes and coolers
- Office supplies for coordination

### Volunteer Your Time

Your time is a valuable donation! See our [Join page](/join) for volunteer opportunities.

## How to Contribute

1. **Financial donations:** Contact us for bank transfer details or donation platforms
2. **Food donations:** Drop off during our distribution hours
3. **In-kind donations:** Reach out to coordinate pickup or delivery

## Transparency

We believe in complete transparency. All donations are tracked and reported to our community. We're proud to say that **90% of all donations** go directly to our programs and community support.
