# Ways to Help

We cook food and hand it out, so the things that help most are the concrete ones: something to cook with, something to put the food in, somewhere to cook it, and a way to move it.

All of it is arranged by email. Write to info@GROUP_DOMAIN, say what you have, and a person will write back to work out the details. There is nowhere to drop things off - we have no premises and no opening hours, so please do not bring anything anywhere until someone has arranged it with you.

## What We Need

### Ingredients

**Ingredients have to be vegan.** Everything we cook is, so anything containing meat, dairy, eggs or honey is no use to us.

### Containers

We need things to put cooked food into so it can be carried and handed over.

### Kitchen time

We need somewhere to cook. If you have a kitchen that could take a group for an afternoon, that is one of the most useful things anyone can offer.

### Transport

Cooked food has to get from where it was made to the people it is for. A vehicle, and someone willing to drive it, closes that gap.

## Your Time

Coming to cook, or coming out when the food is handed over, is help in its own right. The [get involved](/join) page explains how that works.
