# About Our Community Food Group

Founded in 2024, our community food group is a grassroots organization dedicated to ensuring that everyone in our neighborhood has access to nutritious, affordable food.

## Our Mission

We believe that access to healthy food is a fundamental human right. Our mission is to:

- **Connect** people to fresh, local food sources
- **Educate** our community about nutrition and sustainable eating
- **Support** local farmers and food producers
- **Reduce** food waste through smart distribution

## How We Work

We operate through a network of volunteers, local farmers, and community partners. Every week, we:

1. Collect surplus produce from local farms
2. Organize community food distributions
3. Host cooking workshops and nutrition classes
4. Advocate for food justice policies

## Get Involved

Whether you're looking to volunteer, donate, or simply learn more about food security, we welcome you to join our community. No contribution is too small, and every hand makes a difference.
