# Who We Are

We are a mutual aid group in Tucson, Arizona. We get together to cook, and then we take the food out and hand it directly to people around town, including at street locations like bus stops.

Everything we cook and give out is vegan.

## How It Works

We cook and hand food out monthly. We do not publish a time or a place for it on this page, because the details are settled close to the date and they genuinely change - anything printed here in advance would be wrong more often than it was right.

To find out when the next one is, or to come and cook with us, write to info@GROUP_DOMAIN. A person reads that address and will write back.

## The Garden Plot

We rent a large plot at Presidio Garden. The garden is one of the Community Gardens of Tucson, and it is run by them and not by us - we hold a plot in it, and that is the whole of the relationship. A plot there means paying a plot fee and taking out a membership with <a href="https://www.communitygardensoftucson.org/garden-locations" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Community Gardens of Tucson</a>, which is why we publish the address but no directions. It is not somewhere a neighbor can simply turn up.

We go out to the plot every other week during the growing season to keep it going - sometimes more often, sometimes less.

What the plot grows does not sit apart from the rest of this. Some of it is cooked into the food we hand out, and the rest goes straight to people in the group and to neighbors who need it.

## Cooking Classes

We are putting together cooking classes about cheap, healthy meals. They are not running yet. If you would come to one, or you could help teach one, say so when you write to us.

## Coming Along

Anyone who wants to cook, or to help hand food out, is welcome - see [how to get involved](/join). If what you have to offer is ingredients, containers, kitchen time or a vehicle, the [ways to help](/help) page sets out what we need.
