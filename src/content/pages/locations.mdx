# Where We Are

Most of what we do has no fixed address. We cook together and hand the food out around town, monthly, and the arrangements are made close to the date - so there is no place or time here to turn up to. To come along, or to find out where the next one is, write to [info@vegansagainstfascism.org](mailto:info@vegansagainstfascism.org) and a person will write back.
